# Heating Element

## Fuel Is Now an Administrative Expense

For generations, furnace operators accepted the exhausting cycle of locating fuel, loading fuel, and remembering why they
entered the fuel storage room. GenTech rejected this inefficiency and developed the **Heating Element**, an electrically
persuasive surface capable of superheating a furnace using a simple redstone signal.

Place a furnace directly above the Heating Element, then supply the element with redstone power. While powered, it keeps
the furnace lit without consuming fuel. Removing the signal shuts the furnace off, allowing operators to suspend production
for maintenance, emergencies, and scheduled periods of staring at the machinery.

An active Heating Element can also create an upward bubble column through water placed above it. GenTech is pleased to
offer both industrial heating and aquatic vertical transportation from the same entirely reasonable surface.

## Mandatory Thermal Awareness

Following several spontaneous combustion events, GenTech strongly encourages all personnel to keep hands, feet, tools,
loose clothing, and extraneous body parts away from an active Heating Element. For reference, the approved thermal formula
is provided below:

**HEATING = HEAT = FIRE. FIRE BAD.**

Contact with an active element causes damage. The redstone signal is an operating control, not a decorative suggestion,
and smoke is considered a late-stage notification.
