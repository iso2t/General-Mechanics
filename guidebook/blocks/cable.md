# Cables

## Finally, a Use for Network Ports

GenTech machines were designed with advanced Machine Network ports, allowing them to remain professionally isolated from
one another. Following several quarters of disappointing network activity, a vigorous research initiative determined that
the ports would be considerably more useful if something connected them.

After evaluating direct machine contact, strategic shouting, and increasingly long sticks, GenTech researchers identified
**cables** as the preferred solution.

Place cables between network-enabled machines and devices to join them to the same **Machine Network**. Connected machines
can transfer items, blocks, fluids, and energy across the network, subject to the capabilities and configuration of each
machine. Cables automatically connect to neighboring cables and compatible network ports.

Cables have no channel limit. Your network may include as many as your facility requires, provided that “requires” has been
approved by whoever is responsible for navigating the resulting cable infrastructure.

> **GENTECH CONNECTIVITY NOTICE:** An unconnected network port remains fully operational within its current role of
> transferring absolutely nothing.

GenTech congratulates the Infrastructure Division for successfully inventing the space between two machines.
