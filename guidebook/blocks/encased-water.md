# Encased Water

## Hydration, Now Structurally Compliant

Following the success of the Encased Lava program, GenTech engineers asked whether the same enclosure could contain a
material less actively opposed to workplace safety. Encased Water safely contains water inside a durable industrial block,
providing all the approved benefits of water without allowing it to wander freely across company flooring.

Encased Water is not a water source block, but behaves like one when other blocks or systems check for water. It is the
same proven containment principle as Encased Lava, now available with reduced temperatures and significantly less lava.

When relocation becomes necessary, use a stone-tier or better pickaxe to recover the block. Improper extraction may result
in the immediate reclassification of a portable hydration asset as a permanent architectural feature.

> **GENTECH HYDRATION NOTICE:** The water is contained for operational use. Drinking through the casing is not supported.

GenTech guarantees that all enclosed water was wet at the time of installation. Continued wetness may vary by dimension.
