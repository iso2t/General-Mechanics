# Encased Lava

## Portable Heat, Permanently Improved

Following the highly informative Intern Thermal Containment Trial, GenTech engineers developed a safer and considerably
more rectangular method of handling lava. Encased Lava safely contains lava inside a durable industrial enclosure,
allowing valuable thermal material to be handled without requiring an open pit, a handwritten warning sign, or another
intern. It is not a lava source block, but behaves like one when other blocks or systems check for lava.

Encased Lava is used as the thermal core of a **Heating Element**. When relocation becomes necessary, use a stone-tier or
better pickaxe to recover the block. Striking the casing with unapproved equipment may convert company property into a
stationary learning opportunity.

> **GENTECH CONTAINMENT NOTICE:** “Encased” describes the lava's current location. It is not a recommendation to stand on it.

GenTech guarantees that all lava is inside the casing at the time of issue. Continued participation by the casing is the
operator's responsibility.
