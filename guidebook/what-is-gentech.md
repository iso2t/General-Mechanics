# What Is GenTech?

![GenTech Logo](assets/gentech.png)

## A Brief, Mandatory Corporate History

GenTech is a multinational, multidimensional, and occasionally multi-building industrial prosperity organization founded
on one enduring principle:

> **The natural world is simply a factory that has not yet received adequate supervision.**

Before GenTech, rocks remained underground without production targets. Trees grew in unapproved directions. Lava occupied
valuable surface area while contributing nothing to quarterly output. Humanity called this “nature.” GenTech called it an
actionable efficiency crisis.

## The Founding Incident

According to the official company history—most recently revised after the original archive became structurally
unavailable—GenTech was founded by **Dr. Gene T. Echnician**, an engineer, amateur economist, and the only person at the
founding meeting who had brought a clipboard.

While observing a tree growing freely beside an exposed rock formation, Dr. Echnician asked three questions that would
change industry forever:

1. Why has nobody assigned these resources an inventory number?
2. Why is the tree producing wood without submitting a production report?
3. How much energy would it take to make this somebody else's responsibility?

Using borrowed tools, an unauthorized power connection, and several colleagues who had not yet been informed they were
employees, Dr. Echnician established the **General Technology Company** inside a rented storage shed. The shed was
immediately redesignated the **Central Research Campus**, increasing its assessed strategic value by 600%*.

> *This number is not reviewed and not disputable by anyone that is capable of disputing it.

GenTech has repeatedly denied that the company was named after Gene T. Echnician. The approved expansion is
**Generalized Engineering for New-world Transformation, Efficiency, Compliance, and Human-prosperity**. This expansion
was adopted eleven minutes after Marketing learned what an acronym was and several years after the company name had
already appeared on the building.

## Early Prosperity

GenTech's first invention was a metal enclosure containing a lever, two gears, and a written promise that something useful
would eventually happen inside. The device consumed a substantial quantity of power, produced no measurable output, and
was immediately approved as a prototype.

Further research produced the first successful GenTech machine: a powered box capable of accepting one object and
returning a different object after an administratively defensible delay. This established the design philosophy still used
today:

* Inputs should become outputs.
* Outputs should be worth more than inputs.
* Energy costs should be assigned to the facility operating budget.
* Unexpected smoke should be documented before the room is reentered.

Growth followed rapidly. Machines received network ports before anyone had invented a way to connect them. Furnaces were
superheated before researchers agreed on a safe definition of “superheated.” Intern recruitment increased sharply and,
for unrelated reasons, intern retention became a protected internal metric.

By the time the original storage shed lost its final recognizable wall, GenTech had become an industry leader.

## The General Series

As research expanded, GenTech organized its work into the **General Series**, a collection of specialized divisions united
by shared technology, standardized paperwork, and a mutual refusal to accept responsibility for another division's noise.

* **General Mechanics** develops machines, material processing, automation, industrial fluids, and multiblock factories.
  It is considered the practical foundation of all GenTech operations.
* **General Ballistics** was established after a Mechanics prototype exited the laboratory at exceptional speed. Rather
  than classify the event as a containment failure, GenTech recognized an emerging market.
* **General Synthesis** manages agriculture, biology, and advanced arboriculture. It was formed when researchers discovered
  that trees could produce materials without electricity, a claim initially rejected as financially implausible.

Additional divisions may exist. Some remain under development. Others remain under the facility.

## The New-World Prosperity Initiative

GenTech eventually encountered a strategic obstacle: established worlds already contained regulations, property records,
and people who remembered previous demonstrations. The company therefore launched the **New-World Prosperity Initiative**,
an ambitious program to establish productive industry wherever civilization was absent, incomplete, recently exploded,
or otherwise unable to object within the required response period.

Each new facility requires a **Prosperity Asset**: an adaptable individual responsible for gathering resources, building
infrastructure, operating machinery, maintaining power, expanding production, and accepting all recognition associated
with local outcomes.

You were selected for this position through GenTech's advanced candidate-identification process. Specifically, you opened
the guidebook.

Opening company orientation material confirms curiosity, basic hand function, and physical proximity to the work site.
These qualifications exceed the minimum requirements for immediate assignment. Previous employment, technical training,
and informed consent are welcomed but not required.

> **GENTECH ASSIGNMENT NOTICE:** Your title of Prosperity Asset includes the duties of facility director, maintenance
> technician, network administrator, structural engineer, materials specialist, emergency responder, and person currently
> holding the wrench. Compensation is performance-based and may be extracted directly from the surrounding environment.

## GenTech Today

Modern GenTech continues to pursue a future in which every resource has a purpose, every purpose has a machine, and every
machine has enough network ports to create follow-up research opportunities.

The company is privately owned by GenTech Holdings, a wholly owned subsidiary of General Holdings, which is managed by the
GenTech Corporate Ownership Trust on behalf of an organization whose name has been redacted from the ownership diagram.
The diagram is circular, legally valid, and classified as load-bearing.

Our current corporate values are:

* **Prosperity** — measurable improvement for the company and a strong sense of participation for everyone else.
* **Innovation** — solving tomorrow's problems with equipment tested during yesterday's incidents.
* **Safety** — the process by which unexpected outcomes become numbered procedures.
* **Transparency** — unrestricted access to all information approved for unrestricted access.
* **People** — GenTech's most valuable renewable resource.

Despite ongoing developments, current ongoings, and several departments that no longer appear on internal maps, GenTech
remains committed to your success. Your facility may begin with an empty inventory and a patch of wilderness, but with
proper initiative it can become an enormous automated industrial complex containing a slightly smaller patch of
wilderness.

> **GENTECH HISTORICAL ACCURACY NOTICE:** All events described above have been verified against the current approved
> version of company history. Conflicting records are outdated, unofficial, or located in the original archive beneath the
> Central Research Campus.

Welcome to GenTech. We existed before your assignment, we will prosper because of your assignment, and your assignment has
already begun.
