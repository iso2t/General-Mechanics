# Tips and Tricks

* Many multiblocks may share walls (known as wall-sharing in the GTCEu community). This is valid as long as the walls are isometric.
* Optifine may clash with some renderings. General Mods does not support Optifine.
* The wrench has a durability! Breaking blocks and crafting items with it will consume durability. Rotating with the wrench does not consume durability.
* Cables are only able to connect to network-enabled blocks. You must have your block on a Machine Network for the factories to work.
* Cables do not have a channel limit. You can connect as many cables as you want.
