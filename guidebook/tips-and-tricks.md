# Tips and Tricks

## Lessons from Previous Success Assets

At GenTech, every outcome is a successful outcome. Some outcomes improve productivity. Others improve the accuracy of our
safety documentation. The following field-tested recommendations have been recovered from previous personnel and approved
for immediate implementation.

* **Consolidate responsibly.** Multiblocks may share ordinary casing blocks across one or more walls, a practice known as
  wall-sharing in the GTCEu community. Keep hatches off shared casing blocks so every neighboring multiblock remains valid.
* **Use approved visual enhancements.** OptiFine may conflict with some rendering. General Mods does not support OptiFine,
  regardless of how professionally the resulting visual anomaly is reported.
* **Respect wrench durability.** Breaking blocks and using the wrench in crafting will damage it. GenTech recommends
  inspecting your tools before beginning any operation that would become inconvenient halfway through.
* **Network before expecting results.** Cables connect only to network-enabled blocks. A block must be connected to a
  Machine Network before your factories can use it.
* **Expand confidently.** Cables have no channel limit. Connect as many as your facility requires, your layout permits, and
  your ability to remember where they go can reasonably support.

> **GENTECH KNOWLEDGE RETENTION NOTICE:** Repeating a documented mistake does not constitute additional research.
