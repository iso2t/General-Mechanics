# Creosote Oil

## Waste Has Been Reclassified

Creosote Oil is a dark industrial fluid produced alongside Coal Coke in the
[Primitive Coke Oven](../multiblocks/primitive-coke-oven.md). Early GenTech researchers described it as an “unavoidable
oily byproduct.” The Product Development Division removed the first two words, increased the storage budget, and announced
a new revenue stream.

Creosote is used to manufacture treated wood. It is produced automatically during every Coke Oven operation, whether the
operator requested it, planned for it, or has recently discovered that the fluid tank is full.

## Production Yields

| Coke Oven Input | Creosote Oil Produced |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 Coal | 250 mB |
| 1 Coal Block | 2,250 mB |
| 1 Charcoal | 500 mB |
| 1 burnable Log | 250 mB |

The Primitive Coke Oven stores up to **32 buckets** of Creosote Oil internally. If its tank cannot accept the full recipe
output, processing pauses until storage becomes available. GenTech recommends extracting Creosote through a Fluid Output
Hatch or Network Hatch before “the tank is probably fine” becomes a production strategy.

Creosote may be transported in buckets or routed into compatible fluid storage. Buckets should remain upright, sealed,
and clearly separated from any bucket labeled “lunch.”

> **GENTECH FLUID SAFETY NOTICE:** Creosote Oil is intended for industrial wood treatment. It is not a beverage, skin-care
> product, emergency soup base, or substitute for workplace morale.

GenTech proudly converts every unwanted byproduct into a mandatory storage opportunity.
