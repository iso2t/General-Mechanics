# Honey Bun™

## The Taste of Mandated Prosperity

Following several employee-satisfaction surveys with results ranging from **concerning** to **please stop asking**, GenTech
authorized several million dollars for the development of a portable, glazed morale solution. Thus began Project H.O.N.E.Y.:
**Human Optimization through Nutritionally Engineered Yield**.

The project endured countless recipe revisions, multiple glaze-containment failures, and the loss of dozens of voluntold
interns. Their sacrifice was unfortunate, necessary, and represented by a tastefully small footnote in the final budget.
Those who remained prospered, and from their work emerged GenTech's greatest contribution to workplace satisfaction:
the **Honey Bun™**.

## Certified Nutritional Performance

| Evaluated Property | Approved Result |
| --- | --- |
| Hunger Restored | 3 points, or 1.5 hunger icons |
| Saturation Modifier | 0.25 |
| Consumption Eligibility | Always |

Unlike conventional food, the Honey Bun™ may be eaten even when your hunger bar is already full. GenTech understands that
some problems are emotional, some are industrial, and some can be postponed by eating a glazed pastry despite being
physically incapable of benefiting from it.

To consume, hold the Honey Bun™ and use it as you would any food item. Packaging is unnecessary because the protective
glaze has already assumed that responsibility.

> **GENTECH EMPLOYEE WELLNESS NOTICE:** The Honey Bun™ is intended to increase employee satisfaction with all current
> ongoings. It does not alter the current ongoings. Continued awareness of the current ongoings should be reported to your
> supervisor during an approved break period.

GenTech thanks the surviving members of Project H.O.N.E.Y. for proving that prosperity can be mass-produced, lightly
glazed, and distributed one-and-a-half hunger icons at a time.
